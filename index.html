    Small batches, written down

    We roast twelve kilos at a time on a refurbished drum roaster and record the temperature every few seconds. Each coffee has a recipe, and the recipe is a curve.

    If a batch drifts more than a degree from its curve, we dump it and start again. Whatever ships has been roasted within the last 48 hours, so the bag you open is still waking up.
